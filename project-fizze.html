The Concept & Vision

FIZZÉ is an in-house flagship concept project engineered at Growvia Solutions to demonstrate what next-generation D2C e-commerce looks like when high-impact 3D WebGL scrollytelling meets high-conversion direct-to-consumer shopping.

Inspired by global beverage experiences and modern craft mixer brands, the goal was to build a lightning-fast, zero-external-model 3D web experience where every metallic soda can, condensation droplet, ice cube, and fizzy liquid pour is generated procedurally in real time using Three.js and GSAP ScrollTrigger — paired with full e-commerce features and 100/100 technical SEO.

Key Features Engineered

  • 🧊 Procedural 3D Soda Cans (Three.js + HTML5 Canvas): Custom-lathed metallic cans featuring high-DPI wrap-around labels, nutrition facts, barcodes, double-ring pull-tabs, and 100+ glistening condensation droplets without heavy external `.glb` downloads.
  • 🥤 Scroll-Driven 3D Pour Ritual (GSAP ScrollTrigger): Pinned interactive storytelling section where scrolling cracks open the pull-tab, tilts the can, and pours sparkling cola into a refractive glass with bobbing ice cubes, rising foam, and 160+ carbonation bubbles.
  • 🎨 Live 4-Flavour 3D Morphing Switcher: Instant switcher in the hero section that morphs the 3D can texture, studio rim lighting, and entire site accent theme across Classic Cola, Lemon Zest, Orange Pop, and Berry Blast.
  • ✨ "Your Name on the Can" 3D Personalizer: Interactive customizer allowing users to type any name and see it rendered live in 3D (e.g., ★ MADE FOR SAUMYA ★) on the rotating metallic can before adding their custom pack to the cart.
  • 🔄 Interactive 360° 3D Product Cards: Every product card in the store features a live auto-spinning, drag-to-rotate 3D can powered by a single shared offscreen WebGL renderer for butter-smooth 60 FPS performance.
  • 🎬 Shoppable Mixology Video Reels & Recipe Builder: Vertical video reel cards with fullscreen modal playback, step-by-step mocktail recipes, and 1-click "Add Ingredients to Cart".
  • 🔊 Synthesized Web Audio ASMR Engine: Zero-asset procedural sound synthesis generating metallic pull-tab crack, pressurized fizz hiss, liquid pour, and bubble pops using the browser's Web Audio API.
  • 🔍 Technical SEO & WhatsApp Commerce: Complete Open Graph social cards, JSON-LD `Product`, `Brand`, and `FAQPage` schemas, XML sitemap, and a persistent slide-out cart drawer with direct WhatsApp order checkout.
