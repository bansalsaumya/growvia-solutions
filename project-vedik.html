The Challenge

Vedik Realty required a premium, end-to-end digital footprint for the real estate landscape in Dharuhera and NCR. Potential property buyers and investors needed an intuitive platform to browse residential plots, luxury villas, commercial spaces, and high-yield investment options with instant inquiry and location navigation.

The objective was to combine a modern full-stack web application with dominant local SEO rankings on Google, verified Google Business Profile mapping, social media brand visibility on Instagram, and multi-channel lead funnels.

Services Provided

  • 🌐 Full-Stack Website Development: Developed a modern, high-converting property portal with dynamic property listings, search filters, location-wise categorization, and direct lead enquiry modals.
  • 🎨 Premium UI/UX Design: High-end luxury architectural theme with smooth animations, intuitive navigation, responsive cards, and clean typography.
  • 📱 100% Mobile & Desktop Responsive: Flawless experience optimized across smartphones, tablets, laptops, and wide screens.
  • 🔍 SEO & Local Search Domination: On-page SEO, localized keywords for Dharuhera & Rewari real estate, Schema markup, XML sitemaps, meta descriptions, and fast page load times.
  • 📍 Google Business Profile (GBP): Complete optimization with photos, verified address (Sector 19, Dharuhera), categories, reviews management, and call-to-action routing.
  • 📱 Social Media Branding & Creatives: Instagram brand profile setup (@vedikrealty), bespoke property showcase carousel posts, reels editing, and promotional banners.
  • 🎯 Lead Generation Architecture: Integrated WhatsApp click-to-chat, phone call buttons, and automated lead capture form connected to the backend.
