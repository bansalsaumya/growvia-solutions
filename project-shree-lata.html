The Challenge

Shree Lata Gifts & Communication is a multi-category retail store offering brand-new smartphones, verified second-hand devices, mobile accessories, electronic gadgets, stationery, cosmetics, and seasonal gifts. The store needed a high-performance modern digital presence to transition from a traditional brick-and-mortar shop into an omnichannel local retail powerhouse.

The goal was to build a full-featured e-commerce catalog website using Next.js 16, establish dominant local search rankings on Google in Ahmedabad, optimize their Google Business Profile for walk-ins, and build an active Instagram shopping feed with direct WhatsApp order conversions.

Services Provided

  • 🌐 Next.js E-Commerce Catalog Website: Built a fast, modern digital store with 8+ product categories (Mobiles, Earbuds, Smart Watches, Accessories, Power Banks, Chargers, Screen Guards), category filters, flash sale countdown, and instant search.
  • 🎨 Modern Retail UI/UX: Clean corporate e-commerce layout with category tabs, product image galleries, live announcement ticker, and responsive shopping cards.
  • 📱 100% Mobile Responsive Design: Fluid mobile-first shopping experience built for quick smartphone browsing and instant WhatsApp ordering.
  • 🔍 SEO & Local Search Domination: Localized schema markup, sitemap generation, structured headings, meta keywords, and Google Map pack optimization for Vatva and Ahmedabad shoppers.
  • 📍 Google Business Profile Optimization: Complete setup and verification with store photos, operating hours (9:00 AM - 10:00 PM), customer reviews management (4.6★ with 200+ reviews), and direct call/directions routing.
  • 📱 Social Media Branding & Creatives: Instagram brand curation (@shreelatastore), aesthetic product grid showcases, offer carousels, unboxing reels, and gadget festival promotional banners.
  • 💬 WhatsApp Instant Enquiry Integration: Direct "Enquire on WhatsApp" action on every product, bridging online interest straight to counter sales.
