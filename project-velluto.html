The Concept & Vision

CASA VELLUTO is an artisanal 3D WebGL culinary and luxury hospitality showcase engineered at Growvia Solutions. Designed with a sunlit Italian Alabaster, Terracotta & Bronte Pistachio aesthetic, it reimagines how specialty cafés, dessert boutiques, and luxury F&B brands can blend interactive 3D storytelling with instant table reservations and D2C ordering.

Every visual element in the 6-chapter experience — from the diamond-embossed waffle cone, organic ridged gelato scoops, and glossy syrup ribbons to the octagonal Bialetti stovetop Moka pot and porcelain Affogato cup — is sculpted procedurally in real time using Three.js, GSAP ScrollTrigger, and Lenis Smooth Scroll with zero external `.glb` file weight.

Key Features Engineered

  • 🍦 Procedural 3D Triple-Scoop Gelato Cone & Moka Pot (Three.js): Custom-sculpted organic gelato domes with creamy bottom ripples, glossy syrup drips, crushed Bronte pistachio & hazelnut inclusions, 24K gold leaf flakes, and a rotating octagonal stovetop espresso maker.
  • 🎨 Live 6-Flavour Color & Editorial Poster Word Switcher: Clicking any of the 6 signature flavours (Pistacchio di Bronte DOP, Fragola di Bosco, Gianduja Reale, Mango Alphonso Solare, Fior di Latte, or Caffè Ristretto) or ← / → arrows dynamically morphs the 3D top scoop, giant editorial background typography, and the entire page's ambient studio gradient.
  • 🔪 3D X-Ray Molten Core Cutaway & Golden Spoon Bite: Interactive 🔪 X-RAY CORE mode slices the top scoop open in 3D to reveal its glowing liquid pistachio/chocolate heart, while 🥄 TAKE A 3D BITE animates a golden tasting spoon scooping out a live bite mark.
  • ☕ Chapter III Interactive 3D Affogato Bar: Users can pick from 4 live 3D gelato scoops inside the porcelain cup, choose between 3 coffee roasts, and trigger a live "Pour Hot Espresso on Ice Cream" 3D stream animation that melts the scoop into golden crema foam.
  • ✨ Il Tuo Cono — Custom 3D Cone Architect & Polaroid Generator: Full 3-scoop customizer with Vessel selector (Crispy Waffle, Dark Dipped, Brioche), Crown Toppings (24K Gold Leaf, Bronte Crumbs, Amarena Drizzle), 🎲 Surprise Combo, and a 1-click 3D Polaroid Postcard snapshot download.
  • 📅 Flagship Café Table Reservation & WhatsApp Concierge: Interactive booking engine for Mumbai, New Delhi & Bengaluru boutiques that generates an instant digital pass (PASS #CV-XXX) and pre-fills a direct WhatsApp confirmation to +91 7435967985.
  • 📱 100% Mobile-Responsive 3D Framing & Hinglish FAQ: Adaptive portrait camera framing on smartphones, smooth touch scrolling over the 3D canvas, and an interactive Hinglish customer FAQ accordion.
